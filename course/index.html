Organization (UCLouvain Specific)#

Pedagogy#

The pedagogy used is that of Flipped classroom.

Therefore, it is imperative that students work from home:

  • read the mentioned chapters of the reference book,

  • program regularly by doing the exercises offered on INGInious

  • prepare theoretical questions.

It is important that you get the reference book as soon as possible. There are about twenty copies available for rent in BST (Biblioth&egrave;que des Sciences et Technologies).

Mandatory Reference 4th Edition Sedgewick & Wayne

Algorithms (4th Edition)

Robert Sedgewick & Kevin Wayne — Addison-Wesley

Essential for the flipped-classroom format: students are expected to read the designated chapters before each lab session.

🏛️ BST Library Rental: ~20 copies available for student rent in Louvain-la-Neuve.

Agenda#

The course is organized in 6 modules of 2 weeks. Each module spans over an interactive two-week cycle:

Week 1

Acquire Theory

Wednesday 14:00
Lecture with Prof. P. Schaus

Debrief module i-1 & introduction to module i

Thursday 08:30 or 14:00
Lab Session in Computer Room

Hands-on practice with TAs & tutors (Teams support at 08:30)

Rest of Week
Personal Study

Book reading and preparing theoretical exercises A

➔
Week 2

Master Implementation

Wednesday 14:00
Review Session with Prof. P. Schaus

Correction and discussion of exercises A

Thursday 08:30 or 14:00
Lab Session in Computer Room

Hands-on programming with TAs & tutors (Teams support at 08:30)

Rest of Week
Personal Study

Book reading & preparing exercises B on INGInious

🔄
Week 3 (Wednesday 14:00): Correction of exercises B and kick-off of the next module.
